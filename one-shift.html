On the floor

WASD to walk. Move the mouse to look. Shift to run, Space to jump, R to return to the entrance.

Aim at a wall switch and press E or click. E also changes a rack's finish. Escape releases the mouse; P pauses the game.

In the forklift

Walk to the yellow truck and press E. Read the controls, then press Enter. W/S drives forward and reverse; A/D steers the rear wheels. Space brakes, Shift gives a slow approach, and F sets the parking brake.

C switches between the rear and cab views, V centres the view, and H sounds the horn. Stop before pressing E to step out. Hold R while stopped to recover to the parking bay. Reverse one side onto the narrow ramp beside the bay to test its balance. Pallet lifting is still to come.
