Hold the left mouse button to carry a carton while the TC22 is stowed, or hold E in either mode. Release to put it down. Scroll the mouse wheel to bring it closer or move it farther away. While carrying, hold the right mouse button and move the mouse to rotate the carton. It keeps its orientation until you rotate it.

Q equips or stows your TC22. Hold left click to aim the scanner and scan a visible barcode. Right click brings its live screen closer or returns it to your hand. Enter confirms a ready warehouse task. The office WMS manages purchase orders, sales orders, stock and bin history. The Business app handles equipment upgrades. Later, enter your forklift with E. Hold Left Shift. Move the mouse up to raise the forks or down to lower them. The farther the lever is from centre, the faster the forks move. Centre the lever to hold their height. Release Shift to stop and look around.

Drive the forks into a pallet's pockets, then lift. Carry low and turn slowly. Lower onto a rack or the floor, then reverse out. H sounds the horn. Hold R while stopped to recover the forklift.

  1. Open for business

    Latchford Distribution supplies garages, workshops and trade counters. Start with $600 and an empty warehouse, or choose Sandbox for stocked bins and example orders.

  2. Receive and put away

    Book PO-1001 in the office WMS. The courier brings three tape cartons to REC-01. Use Receive on your TC22, then Transfer each box into floor storage. Scan the source, carton and destination, set it down and confirm.

  3. Pick and ship

    Release SO-2001 in the office. Pick its allocated cartons into STAGE-01. When the collection van opens, use Ship to scan and load each carton into DISPATCH-01. Step clear and confirm shipment. Payment arrives after departure.

  4. Keep stock in its place

    Every rack position is a bin. Scan labels for Item lookup, and use Transfer when moving stock. The office tracks available stock, allocations, orders and location exceptions. New orders and upgrades open as your business grows.
