Smooth favours frame rate. Sharper favours detail.

On the floor

WASD to walk. Move the mouse to look. Shift to run, Space to jump, R to return to the entrance.

Aim at a wall switch and press E or click. Escape releases the mouse; P pauses the game.

In the forklift

Press E near the yellow forklift to get in. On your first drive, read the short briefing and press E again. W/S drives forward and reverse; A/D steers. Space brakes, and Shift gives a slow approach. The parking brake releases when you drive.

Hold X and move the mouse up or down to raise or lower the forks. Keeping the mouse still holds their height. Release X to look around again. WASD still moves the truck while X is held.

Holding X also reveals the forks and nearby pallet edges through the mast and load. Line the forks up with the pallet pockets, drive them in, then lift clear. Lower onto the floor or rack and reverse out. The pallet's weight and balance still act on the forklift.

Q/Z raises and lowers the forks; T/G tilts the mast. Use these to reach the higher racks. C switches the rear and cab views. E brings the forklift to a stop and steps out when there is room. Tab shows the extra controls.
