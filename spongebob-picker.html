      How does this become the public lineup?

      The public fight post uses a saved lineup. Download your choices and give that file to the person updating the site. Your browser selection does not change the public cast. A fight link carries your chosen characters, so someone else can vote on the same set.

      Where the pictures came from

      This catalog includes characters with a documented appearance in the original SpongeBob SquarePants TV series. Movie, spinoff, game and book characters without a TV-series appearance are excluded. Names, episode references and pictures come from Encyclopedia SpongeBobia. Each card links to its source page.
