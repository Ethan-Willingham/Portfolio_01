All Four Wheels is my isometric shopping cart game. Turn the basket while it keeps sliding in the old direction, then push to bend its path. Six rooms you drive between without stopping, colored floors, shelves that topple, bottles that break, lakes you can fall into, and cliff edges that do not forgive a late brake. Still in progress.

How to play

Get through the store before time runs out. Cones add two seconds to your time, loose boxes add three, and spilled shelves add five.

In the first room, push straight for a moment, let go of W, and hold D. The basket turns while the cart keeps traveling in its old direction. Push again to pull that slide into a curve. Space brakes; S pulls you backward.

On a phone, use the two thumb pads. Slide the left pad sideways to rotate. Slide the right pad up to push or down to pull. Small movements give you a gentle nudge; slide farther for more force. Let go to coast, or hold the middle Brake button to slow down. You can push and turn together.

A close pass by a shelf or cone earns a Close call once you're safely clear. Keep rolling through a half turn for a 180, or finish the whole rotation for a 360. A sustained sideways coast earns a Power slide. Link clean moves within five seconds for a style multiplier, up to four times. Bumps and falls break the combo. Tricks return a little time to the clock, up to twelve seconds per trip. Spinning in place doesn't count.

The gold pins are the swivel pivots. Each tire sits behind its pin on a little fork. Pull backward and watch the wheels swing around; turn while moving and each one follows a different curve. You can see them through the basket.

The thin line ahead of the cart shows where you're moving. The basket shows where you're facing. Those directions can be very different. The map edge and your outline turn red when the basket, a wheel, or you touch the boundary.

Touch the numbered circles in order with any of the four tires. The tire itself counts, including its edges, as it swivels around the gold pin. Clear a room's markers to open the next door. Keep driving. The same cart, momentum, and mess carry through the corridors until the final checkout.

Blue lakes have no floor under the water. The striped cliff edges have no guardrail. Two unsupported wheels, or your body moving beyond the floor, start a fall. You'll return to that room's entrance with eight seconds added to your time. A wheel hanging over an edge turns red before the cart goes over.

The first room starts with one small table and a vase of water. Bump the table and the vase slides off, breaks, and leaves a clear puddle on the tiles. A bump at normal driving speed can rock a shelf, throw its stock off, or tip the whole rack onto the floor. Wine bottles and dishes break; cans roll and bounce. Run a wheel over a ketchup bottle and it squeezes sauce onto the tiles. Wine spreads farther than ketchup, and oily spills make braking harder. The tires pick up water and other spills and leave wet or colored tracks. A table or shelf costs five seconds once, however many things fall.

Three marks for a clean run under the trip's target time. Two for finishing with at most two mishaps. One for getting there. Practice mode has no deadline and doesn't change your records.
