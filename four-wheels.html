The long way round

All Four Wheels is my shopping cart game. An empty cart, four swivel wheels, and a dirt track that keeps taking you through grocery stores. Turn the basket while it slides in the old direction, then push to bend its path. See how far you can get. Still in progress.

How to play

Follow the red and white curbs. Dirt slows the wheels, asphalt carries a slide farther, and the grass shoulders drag on the cart. Some bends have guardrails. Some have a long drop.

Push straight for a moment, let go of W, and hold D. The basket turns while the cart keeps traveling in its old direction. Push again to pull that slide into a curve. Space brakes; S pulls you backward. Brake before a tight bend and leave room for the shopper behind the handle.

On a phone, slide the left thumb pad sideways to rotate. Slide the right pad up to push or down to pull. Small movements give you a gentle nudge. Let go to coast, or hold the middle Brake button to slow down. You can push and turn together.

The route runs through three grocery stores. Push the swinging doors open, or hit them hard enough to break the glass. Tables rock, shelves topple, bottles break, and cans roll. A vase leaves clear water; a wheel over a ketchup bottle squeezes sauce onto the tiles. The tires pick up spills and leave tracks. That mess stays after a fall.

The gold pins are the swivel pivots. Each tire sits behind its pin on a little fork. Pull backward and watch all four swing around. Any tire touching a numbered route circle clears it. The little GO sign points toward the next stretch when it is outside the view.

Two wheels losing the floor, or the center of the cart and shopper going over an edge, starts a fall. You land back at an earlier safe stretch. Some falls cost a little ground. The narrow section without rails can send you all the way back to the start. Your furthest distance stays recorded.

There is no countdown. The run saves automatically in your browser, including the cart, broken stock and puddles. Reach a section to unlock it in the route menu for practice. Practice parks your real run and leaves its record alone. Start over keeps your distance record but begins with a fresh cart and course.

Close passes, moving 180s and 360s, and sustained sideways coasts earn style. Link clean moves within five seconds for a multiplier up to four times. Bumps and falls break the combo. Spinning in place doesn't count. At the end, get the cart, all four tires and the shopper onto the checked finish pad.
