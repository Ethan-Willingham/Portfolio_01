Changes: exclude the Sun, select apparent visual magnitude <= 6.0, sort by
magnitude, quantize coordinates and photometry, round proper motions to whole
milliarcseconds per year, and derive display RGB from measured B-V color
indices. Unknown B-V is retained as missing and given a neutral display color.
