About the gallery

Modern art against AI.
Made entirely by AI.

The works take aim at AI's borrowed creativity, polished mistakes and empty promises. AI generated the pictures, prompts and gallery code; a human set the brief and chose what stayed.

The wall repeats a finite collection. It does not generate new images while you browse.
