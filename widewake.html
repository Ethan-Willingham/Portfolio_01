Your voyage

The captain's handbook

Make a living at sea

The helm

WASD or arrows steer. Hold Shift for full steam. Click and hold on the ocean to aim your guns. Scroll to zoom.

Drag the steering pad to sail. Push it to the rim for full steam. Your guns aim and fire automatically. Hold a second finger on open water to aim and fire yourself. Use + and − to zoom.

Open the world chart to choose a destination. The helmsman follows a water route. Steer to take over whenever you want. Your destination stays marked; open the chart to set a fresh course from your new position.

A profitable hold

Your first 12 sacks of sugar are already aboard. Nassau pays well for them. Open Trade there and sell your cargo.

Buy local exports, then find a port that wants them. Order buttons show the total price. Choose a good’s name to compare buyers and sailing times on the chart. You can review and buy a new order there before departure. Quotes can change while you sail. Workboat holds reserve space for fish or oil; change this in Convoy while docked.

Keep the fleet afloat

Guns and escorts fire automatically. Broadside fires every gun at once, then needs nine seconds to reload. Patch hulls repairs the fleet for 100 coins.

Use Convoy to assign guards to particular boats. Red hull bars need attention. A sunken support boat takes its cargo with it. If your flagship goes down, salvagers take half the remaining cargo and a recovery fee. Harbor relief keeps at least 260 coins available; surviving hulls are repaired. Your progress and upgrades remain.

Work and discovery

A trawler fishes; an oil tender pumps crude. Bring one to the matching site and deploy it. Protect it until the operation finishes. Check the chart for the working time, expected load and raider forecast. Cold-water schools move; deep nets and pressure wells bring larger loads up in slower cycles. Approach warnings give you time to reposition. Recall a workboat early to keep the cargo already aboard, or finish the watch for its bonus.

Sea signals mark wrecks, traders and crews in trouble. The Voyage desk links your first trade to a journey around the world. Follow it at your own pace.
