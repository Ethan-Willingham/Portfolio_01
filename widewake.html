How to play

Sail

WASD Steer
Shift Full steam
H Autopilot
Mouse wheel: zoom.

Drag the pad to steer. Push to the rim for full steam. Tap + or minus to zoom.

Choose a destination on the chart. Steer to take over.

Trade

Buy local exports. Sell where demand is high.

Select goods to see prices and compare ports. Workboats reserve cargo space for fish or oil.

Fight

Guns aim and fire automatically. Hold on open water to aim.

Broadside fires every gun. Repair costs 100c. Assign escorts in Fleet.

Sunken boats lose their cargo. A sunk flagship is rescued for a fee and half your remaining cargo.

Work

Bring a trawler to fishing grounds or an oil tender to oil fields. Protect it while it works.

Finish a watch to unlock a longer one. Recall keeps loaded cargo. Rare catches pay when you dock.
