Sources & accuracy

Clouds: NASA and EUMETSAT satellite images, sampled every 15 minutes. The globe checks for updates every five minutes. Infrared also shows clouds at night. Processing delays and outages can leave old images or gaps.

Aurora: NOAA's forecast, checked every minute. Replay uses saved forecasts. The glow's height and brightness are illustrated; clouds and daylight can hide a real aurora.

Stars: HYG, David Nash (CC BY-SA 4.0). City lights: NASA, 2016. Sun and Moon distances are compressed.
