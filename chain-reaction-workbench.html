Chain Reaction

One marble sets the next small accident in motion.

4 stages / 24 transfers / About 35 seconds

Maple arms tip, tin cups swing, and a falling weight pulls open a drawer. The chain travels through a workshop, sewing alcove, toy theatre and curiosity cabinet. The separate studies are below. It's still being built.

Watch the four-stage chain
1. The Long Arm 2. A Small Weight 3. One More Turn 4. The drawer opens

On the bench

These are separate mechanisms and construction studies. The rocker is a candidate for the next stage. Sound is available in its own comparison lab.

  • Catch and return

    A lifting tray catches the incoming marble. A gravity timer opens the latch and sends that same marble on its way.

  • Drawer outlet

    The fourth stage on its own. One marble releases a weight, the weight opens the drawer, and the drawer's cord releases the next marble.

  • Rocker outlet

    A maple tray tilts under a marble, pulls an outlet cord, then returns under its counterweight. It hasn't joined the chain yet.

  • Rocker landing

    The earlier rocker study. A brass stop holds the marble until the tray lowers it into a felt landing cradle.

  • Drawer arrival

    A marble touches a wooden trip, which withdraws the support beneath the drawer's falling weight.

  • Drawer bearings

    The first drawer study: a tin weight pulls the drawer along its brass slides until it reaches the stop.

  • Woodshop construction

    Five props in two poses each: a sliding drawer, folding square, felt cradle, hinged arrow and pencil rack.

  • Sound comparison

    Try the material voices beside the moving machine. Sound remains off in the main chain while these are being tuned.

  • Earlier three-stage slice

    The arm, cup and wheel before the drawer joined them. Kept here for comparison with the current draft.
