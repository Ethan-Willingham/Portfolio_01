The route clock measures watching time. A room's solver clock has its own units. Moving the route slider chooses a room and starts it from its seed; it does not seek a simulation into the past.

        

      

      

        
A soap film, a superfluid, a hydrogen atom and a gauge field can share a frame without sharing an equation. Descent moves between them with dark rests. The journey is editorial; each room keeps its own clock, colors and scale.

How this prototype works
The proposed route spends 170 seconds in each room, with ten seconds for fades and a rest. A measured event may extend a stay to 210 seconds. Transitions change presentation, never solver coefficients.
The interim hydrogen study evolves a normalized 1s and 2p state analytically. Brightness encodes probability density, not emitted light. Its 36-second beat is slowed for watching. The no-WebGPU image is a CPU calculation of that same state at zero phase.
Rooms draw linear radiance into a shared format. Descent applies one Reinhard display mapping and one sRGB transfer. It adds no bloom, fluid warp, dither or sound. Repeated visits begin with a seed derived from the route cycle. GPU replay may differ across devices. The analytic study shares a time law, but visitors are not synchronized to a world clock.
