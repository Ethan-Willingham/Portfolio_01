Hunting Game is about watching a field while the day rushes past. Hold Fast-forward and watch the sun cross the sky. Wildlife briefly eases the speed. Release in time to take a closer look, or keep holding and let it pass. Still in progress.

Controls and shooting

Click once to raise the scope, then move the mouse to aim. Small movements near the center keep the view steady. Move the sight toward the lens edge to look farther across the field; bring it back toward the center to stop. Click again to shoot. Q or right-click lowers the scope. On a phone, drag on the field to aim, tap Aim to raise the scope, then Shoot. Hold F, Space, or Fast-forward to pass time. Release to return to normal speed. P pauses.

Aim above the shoulder to allow for bullet drop, and against the wind to allow for drift. Farther shots need more correction. The scope stays up while the round travels; the small field view shows its whole flight. You have five rounds, with automatic reloading after the last shot. R reloads sooner.

Deer and boar pass through the field. Squirrels move through the grass, and owls cross overhead. A vital hit saves a recovery after a short run. A body hit can send the animal into the trees. Your clock and recoveries save in this browser.
