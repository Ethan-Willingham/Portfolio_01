Aim beyond the deer along your sightline. Hold right mouse for the scope. P pauses. No timer, keep practicing.

Hunting Game started as a Unity project I made with AI. I don't want to keep developing it in Unity, so this is the first JavaScript version: the same stand, the same pixel art, and a round you can watch fall through the field. Still in progress.

How shooting works

Your crosshair marks where the round would land without wind. Aim at a deer's hooves and the round arrives too low. Aim beyond the deer, along the line from your stand, and the round is still above the ground when it reaches the animal. Aim too far beyond it and you shoot over its back.

With the guide on, the gold ring marks the vitals near the front shoulder. The dotted line is the visible bullet's predicted path with the current wind. Bring that line through the ring, then wait for the deer to stop walking or lead its movement. The little square marks the predicted landing point. The guide won't move your aim or correct the shot.

Transparent pixels between the legs or around the antlers let a round pass through. A hit outside the vitals wounds the deer and it runs. A vital hit brings it down after a short run and counts as recovered. A wounded deer that escapes into the trees doesn't count.

You have five rounds. R reloads; clicking with an empty magazine starts a reload too. Q toggles the scope, or hold the right mouse button. A and D shuffle along the stand. Hold F or the Wait button to move time forward. P pauses. On a phone, drag your aim across the field, then use Fire. New outing resets the field and counters.

Built with JavaScript and a 320 by 180 canvas. The deer, trees, grass, bushes, and hunter are from the Unity prototype. Shooting and deer behavior · Renderer · Game source.
