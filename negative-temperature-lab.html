Fixed offline seed. Apply it with Restart. Opens during the first paddle sweep, after 12,000 computed steps. Floating-point replay can diverge across GPUs.

Model configuration

          

          
Energy includes the moving paddles, which do work on the field. Real-time evolution has no damping or norm correction. Candidate annihilations are estimates between samples.

          
Warm circles mark positive winding; blue circles mark negative winding. Phase hue is a smooth, constant-lightness OKLab color wheel, an encoding rather than emitted light.

        

      

      

        
Each dark core carries one turn of the condensate’s phase. Two repulsive paddles move through the field, shed vortices, and withdraw. The wave equation then decides what survives. Same-sign cores can organize into persistent groups; Instruments measures their circulation and organization.

        
“Negative temperature” refers to the statistical temperature of the vortex subsystem. This prototype does not estimate that temperature or establish thermodynamic equilibrium. The condensate is a two-dimensional mean-field model, with dimensionless lengths and time.
