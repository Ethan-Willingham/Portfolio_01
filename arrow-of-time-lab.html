Energy couples both time layers. Entropy is an 8 × 8 block estimator. The zoom stops at resolved cells; the display has no trails. No equilibrium criticality or fractal dimension is claimed.

The hourglass comes back, down to its last spin. Two layers of a rectangular lattice evolve by one reversible rule: when four neighbors split two and two, a spin in the older layer flips. The image dissolves into moving domains. After three minutes the rule runs backward, and both layers return exactly. Instruments checks the complete state.

Gold and green show the mean of both time layers. Fine block averages keep the cell detail visible, and brief transitions between computed states soften the motion. The microscopic information survives even when the coarse image disappears; this authored orbit is not a thermal sample.
