# Do You Get Used to Everything? Editorial review

## Reader payoff

The reader can try an honest fixation experiment and distinguish sensory adaptation, habituation, sensitization, tolerance, hedonic change and practiced recognition. The practical payoff is knowing why familiarity alone does not predict relief, enjoyment, competence or life satisfaction. The revised opening uses the lasting effect of lottery wealth as a concrete counterexample to a universal happiness baseline.

## Complete reading and original issues

Read the whole original essay, its 54-item source list, all four SVG figures and accessible descriptions, and the complete root JavaScript. The original main path moved from vision through drug effects, life satisfaction, consumer advice, expected rewards, disasters, skill and art. It repeatedly treated these as one mechanism, then offered the same conclusion several times. Its source section claimed that every assertion had primary support and described a research process whose provenance was unavailable.

The most consequential errors were these:

- The fixation widget deliberately faded its dots with CSS, reported `gone` after 6.2 seconds, and treated pointer movement as equivalent to an eye movement. It therefore demonstrated an animation while describing it as the reader's perception.
- The sensory opening asserted that anything perfectly still disappears and that the senses report only change. The sea-slug example extended a particular reflex pathway to humans generally. Sensitization became habituation in reverse.
- Opponent-process theory was presented as a simple mechanism for every strong feeling, including grief. The addiction discussion generalized from drug research to ordinary rewards, and an overdose example used an incorrectly identified citation.
- The life-event chart gave marriage a two-year recovery clock and widowhood, disability and unemployment a permanent deficit. The German survey's entire population was conflated with its eligible marriage sample. Disability estimates were presented as more settled than the original studies allow.
- Commuting was called an experience people never adapt to. A regression-based income comparison became an amount that could offset a commute. Predictability and control were promoted into a universal rule with an unsupported sorting table.
- Small pleasure and writing experiments were expanded into durable happiness, relationship and dopamine-reset advice. The reward-task model was expanded into daily feelings across whole lives. The two fictional trajectories also contradicted their caption by crossing while one supposedly stayed higher throughout.
- Organizational decisions, moral concern and perceptual learning were described as the same neural machinery. The practice chart invented a meaningful crossing point between quantities with different units. The art conclusion promised near-universal adaptation and described criticism as neuroscience.
- The Clark et al. entry linked to IZA discussion paper 1278, which is the commuting paper. The Ericsson entry linked to an unrelated PMC article. Removing the corresponding tangents removes those misleading links.

## Central claim and source ledger

All retained research claims were narrowed to the relevant design and outcome. Full source access means the original text or author manuscript was opened and the relevant methods, results and limits were read. Reviews and theories are identified as such, rather than described as experiments.

| Claim or material | Evidence actually accessed | Final scope and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Habituation differs from sensory adaptation and fatigue. | [Rankin et al., 2009](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2754195/), full definition review. | Reduced behavioral responding; recovery and stimulus specificity help identify the process. This is a review of criteria. |
| A peripheral target can fade during fixation; microsaccades vary around visibility changes. | [Martinez-Conde et al., 2006](https://smc.neuralcorrelate.com/files/publications/martinez-conde_et_al_neuron06.pdf), original nine-page paper. | Eight observers, eye tracking and reported visibility. The association supports the explanation without measuring the website reader's eyes. |
| Sea-slug reflex habituation can involve reduced transmission between identified cells. | [Castellucci and Kandel, 1974](https://www.pnas.org/doi/pdf/10.1073/pnas.71.12.5004), full five-page original. | Repeated stimulation in isolated ganglia; transmitter release declined while postsynaptic sensitivity remained unchanged. No general human mechanism is inferred. |
| Increased responsiveness in pain pathways contributes to some chronic pain. | [Woolf, 2011](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3268359/), full clinical and experimental review. | Central sensitization is one contributory mechanism. No claim of universal causation, permanence, or voluntary control. |
| Repeated exposure can strengthen an opposing after-reaction in a model of motivation. | [Solomon, 1980](https://www.appstate.edu/~steelekm/classes/psy5300/Documents/Solomon1980_Opponent.pdf), full theory paper. | One model of aspects of tolerance and withdrawal, represented schematically. Grief and universal emotional timelines were removed. |
| Drug-related wanting and pleasure can diverge. | [Berridge and Robinson, 2016](https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/berridge-lab/wp-content/uploads/sites/743/2019/10/2016-Berridge-Robinson-Liking-wanting-IS-theory-of-addiction-Am-Psychol-1.pdf), full author manuscript. | Incentive-sensitization account of addiction. No claim about phones, universal craving or inevitable relapse. |
| The 1978 lottery result did not establish return to a common baseline. | [Brickman et al., 1978](https://gwern.net/doc/psychology/1978-brickman.pdf), full original paper. | 22 winners, 29 injured respondents and 22 controls, interviewed once. Retrospective reports do not supply prospective pre-event measures. |
| Average marital satisfaction changes differ between events and people. | [Lucas et al., 2003](https://digitalcitizen.ca/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/dbdd7-reactionstochangeinmaritalstatus2002.pdf), full original article. | 15 survey waves; marriage analysis restricted to 1,761 people who married and remained married. Recovery has no exact deadline. |
| Disability can show partial recovery with a remaining average loss in satisfaction. | [Oswald and Powdthavee, 2008](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/soc/economics/staff/ajoswald/download/23decospowdisability2007.pdf), full accepted manuscript. | Observational British panel and fixed-effects analysis. The authors dispute earlier recovery estimates; individual outcomes remain undetermined. |
| Lottery wealth can have lasting effects on life satisfaction. | [Lindqvist, Östling and Cesarini, 2018](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w24667/w24667.pdf), full working paper; [published article, 2020](https://doi.org/10.1093/restud/rdaa006). | Random prize comparisons among 3,362 surveyed players, 5 to 22 years later. One follow-up survey; smaller affective effects. Generalization and response selection remain limits. |
| Longer commutes were associated with lower satisfaction. | [Stutzer and Frey, 2008](https://www.bsfrey.ch/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/stress-that-doesnt-pay-the-commuting-paradox.pdf), full published paper and [original working version](https://docs.iza.org/dp1278.pdf). | Within-person panel association. No claim of zero adaptation or experimentally established income compensation. |
| A one-week chocolate break increased enjoyment at retasting. | [Quoidbach and Dunn, 2013](https://dunn.psych.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/combatting_hedonic_adaptation.pdf), full manuscript. | Randomized conditions; 55 students completed both visits. One food, one week, self-reported savoring and positive affect. |
| Writing about a positive event's possible absence can affect immediate feelings. | [Koo et al., 2008](https://dtg.sites.fas.harvard.edu/KOO%20ALGOE%20WILSON%20GILBERT%20(2008).pdf), full original paper. | Immediate outcomes. The relationship study selected relatively happy, established couples; no lasting relationship benefit is promised. |
| Recent rewards and expectations predicted momentary happiness in gambling tasks. | [Rutledge et al., 2014](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1407535111), full original article. | Task-specific computational model, including 18,420 smartphone players earning points. Neither earnings nor a life trajectory alone identifies happiness. |
| Familiar warnings and launch decisions can be compared with getting used to something. | [Rogers Commission, 1986, chapter V](https://www.nasa.gov/history/rogersrep/v1ch5.htm), full official chapter. | An optional organizational comparison. The report documents active objections and communication failures, not a measured habituation mechanism. |
| Familiar chess patterns support reconstruction. | [Chase and Simon, 1973](https://iiif.library.cmu.edu/file/Simon_box00005_fld00354_bdl0001_doc0001/Simon_box00005_fld00354_bdl0001_doc0001.pdf), original archival manuscript. | Three participants, real and random boards, brief exposure. A domain-specific illustration, with no general-memory guarantee. |
| Consistent practice can reduce attention demands; changed target roles can cause interference. | [Shiffrin and Schneider, 1977](https://psych.indiana.edu/documents/shiffrin-and-schneider-1977.pdf), full original paper. | Controlled visual-search experiments, including four new participants in the first experiment. The chart is schematic; its crossing has no measured significance. |
| Habit can reduce the attention required for familiar acts. | [James, 1890, chapter IV](https://psychclassics.yorku.ca/James/Principles/prin4.htm), primary text. | Historical account, identified beside the short quotation. No claim that James demonstrated a modern neural mechanism. |
| Art can make familiar things perceptually unfamiliar. | [Shklovsky, 1917](https://catalogimages.wiley.com/images/db/pdf/9781119622604.c01.2.pdf), primary essay in translation. | Critical interpretation. The street-painting example is explicitly hypothetical; no owner experience or clinical effect was invented. |

The 1970 Castellucci paper's cached PubMed abstract was available, but direct access returned a disclosure-only page and the Science endpoint failed. The final version uses the fully opened 1974 PNAS experiment instead. The original disability and H.M. routes had limited or failed access; the final text uses the accessible original disability manuscript and removes the H.M. tangent. Publisher restrictions on the published Swedish lottery article were bypassed through its public NBER working paper, not treated as full published-paper access.
