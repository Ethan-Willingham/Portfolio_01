# Photographs and how they were made: completed editorial review

Reviewed and revised through October 4, 2026. Assigned files only: `archive/best-photographs/best-photographs.html` and this review. No staging, commit, push, shared CSS, search index or progress-ledger edits.

## Reader payoff and coverage

A reader can compare how photographs record motion, color, transmission, distant objects and printed surfaces, and recognize the particular processing that produced each displayed image. The payoff is carried by photographs and working comparisons, with concise explanations beside them. The collection keeps all 63 numbered entries, all 90 main-content image elements with their original image paths, eleven themed rolls and twelve supplementary stories. I read the whole original page, source notes, captions, diagram labels, control text and inline scripts, then reread the revised entries and final display labels.

The principal path is the introduction, contact sheet, numbered entries, captions, technical field notes and comparisons. Six closed disclosures contain the twelve shorter historical and technical stories. A seventh disclosure contains the bibliography and rights notes. A citation opens that source disclosure and scrolls to its source. The large panoramas remain deliberate horizontal viewing areas inside a page that does not overflow.

## Original problems and implemented changes

The original mixed interesting photographic methods with repeated amazement, categorical firsts, unsupported superlatives and an exhortation to buy or use a better camera. It also treated unlike objects as interchangeable: historical negatives and later prints, a source mosaic and its small web reproduction, a camera’s pixel count and a mosaic’s total, or a scanned picture and a digital-camera exposure. Those confusions now receive the explanation beside the relevant photograph.

The biggest concrete corrections concern condensation versus a photographed sound barrier; repeated-event versus single-shot ultrafast reconstruction; three filtered Prokudin-Gorskii records; infrared versus visible views of the pillars; Cassini’s selected mosaic and brightness adjustments; Rubin’s instrument versus released mosaic; the rover’s assembled selfie; the Bayer example; the reconstructed and retouched historical images; Getty’s Steichen photogravure; Boutan’s sitter; the Whipple paper print; Brownie sales and the later photographed camera; camera shipments; and the actual 2026 return to crewed lunar travel.

I removed the present comprehensive auction ranking, unsupported Peter Lik accusations, estimated global daily photograph counts, categorical portrait and popularity rankings, unverified exact auction and camera specifications, and repeated generic closing sermons. Important image construction, source rights and measurement limits remain. Trivial defensive endings were cut, including the bear’s supposed superiority, what a balloon photograph cannot establish about bird vision, and what Hine’s worker cannot prove about his employer. The last numbered entry now closes the gallery, with the small attributed Sagan quotation preceding a final concrete account of the camera’s unresolved Earth and internally scattered sunlight.

The title, social title and h1 are now “Photographs and how they were made.” The description names gallery entries rather than implying that each numbered technical illustration is a separate photograph. The religious-post hero contract supplies the large centered title, upright standfirst and reading metadata; the opening photograph follows the hero. Plain prose uses Century Supra at 1.27rem and 1.72 line height, with a roughly 64-character measure where the layout permits it. Existing photographic mattes, photographs, zone grays, SVG colors, dark gallery bands and other protected colors were not recolored.

## Evidence categories and claim ledger

“Observation” means an inspectable feature of the displayed image. “Mechanism” or “model” means a sourced explanation of acquisition, optics, chemistry, encoding or exposure. “Interpretation” means a reading of framing or composition, expressed without pretending to measure a viewer’s response. Historical dates, counts and changing records are checked separately. Illustrative playback, diagrams and display scales are named as illustrations. No personal memories, professional expertise or owner beliefs were invented.

The sources below were read for the relevant evidence and method, using institutional pages, original papers, primary books or source-object metadata. A museum or archive’s own history is preferred to an unsourced secondary summary. Where an item page blocked retrieval, its catalogue metadata or originating credit was read through its Commons file record; that is identified under residual limits. Source anchor IDs remain stable, but displayed footnote numbers now run consecutively through the 96 used bibliography entries.
