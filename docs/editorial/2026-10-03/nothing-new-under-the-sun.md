# Nothing New Under the Sun: editorial review

Status: complete, with the source limits below. Owned files are `archive/nothing-new-under-the-sun/nothing-new-under-the-sun.html` and this report. No shared files, staging, commits or pushes were performed.

## Reader payoff and reading path

The reader gets a smaller, defensible version of the original ethical argument: old complaints and related moral instructions deserve attention, while happiness studies cannot establish equal inner lives or make circumstances irrelevant. Caring for another person remains the author's commitment, rather than a treatment guaranteed to reward him.

The main path has seven sections, five retained images and the qualitative three-influence illustration. Seven native disclosures hold the five interactive tools, the historical comparison and the research notes. Every tool's generated prose was reviewed, including unselected branches. The title remains a reference to Ecclesiastes, explicitly identified in the ending.

## Original problems and changes

The original treated the human emotional range as an unchanged, equal instrument; presented a two-dimensional theory as consensus; inferred longitudinal recovery from one-time interviews; equated a Forbes comparison with a Maasai survey; and supplied invented event-specific adaptation and happiness forecasts. Its ending repeated those conclusions after caveats had already contradicted them. The science appeared to prove that generosity was the only lever worth moving.

The revision removes that universal claim, the speculative percentages and the personal predictions. It keeps the ethical judgment visible as a judgment. The historical comparison now identifies the different instruments and the unresolved 5.7 versus 5.4 discrepancy. The income slider demonstrates logarithmic arithmetic. The tradition selector presents twelve sourced paraphrases with context, without inventing origin dates or independent transmission histories. Unverified almanac cases were cut rather than left looking authoritative.

The shared religious-text hero is used with title, upright dek and reading metadata in order. The Munch image follows the hero. Main paragraphs use Century Supra at 1.27rem and 1.72 line height, with a roughly 64ch measure, centered headings, fine rules and 20px phone gutters. UI typefaces, the palette, assets and image source links are retained. Research notes are readable at 1rem rather than three columns of 0.52rem text. An ordinary-motion, JavaScript-free reading bug was corrected by restricting reveal hiding to `.js .reveal`.

## Five VOICE passes and length

1. **Monotone read:** read the complete main prose, captions, reference notes, labels and every dataset branch flat. Removed the emotional-ceiling performance, heroic “one lever” claims and repeated dramatic verdicts. The particular study designs carry the research sections.
2. **Delete the first paragraph:** tested the opening without it. The attribution payoff was lost, so it stayed in a shorter form with the 1907 fact. The general complaint-warning paragraph was deleted instead; it repeated the next paragraph's point.
3. **Generic-sentence pass:** replaced claims about science always winning with named papers and measures; removed broad statements about every generation, all people and a rule being true because it recurs. Cut an invented personal response to old paintings. First-person wording is reserved for the ethical commitment and interpretation.
4. **Ten-percent cut:** the first rewritten draft had about 3,134 words across main text, reference text and dataset branches. The cut pass reduced that to about 2,808, slightly over ten percent, without losing the study limits or tools. Final minor UI/caption edits leave approximately 2,805 under the counting convention below.
5. **Source pass:** checked consequential numbers and their measures, controls and inference limits. Experimental inference, observational association, theory, illustrative math and belief are identified where used. The strongest counterexamples to equal well-being and universal adaptation remain in the main path.
