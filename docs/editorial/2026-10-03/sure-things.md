## Reader payoff and editorial decision

The reader gets 18 specific questions with enough evidence and mechanism to understand what is actually unsettled. The old title confused empirical evidence, incomplete mechanisms, philosophical skepticism and formal unprovability. A reliable physical prediction does not require absolute logical certainty; an unfinished theory does not make its successful parts inexplicable. The new opening uses the bicycle's modeled recovery as a concrete example and immediately distinguishes physical unknowns from Gödel's proved limitation.

The four groups and every original subject remain. Each entry now has a question heading, one explanation, its retained image and a caption, and substantive source links beside the claims. There is no arbitrary length target. The available space goes to mechanism and scope, including the placebo trial's outcome, the cryptochrome experiment's laboratory conditions, and the assumptions behind a cosmological age. The repetitive quotation-and-mystery structure, universal ignorance claims and multiple closing paragraphs were removed. No memories, field experience or owner expertise were invented. The original disclosure that an AI helped draft the page and collect its images remains.

## Practical claim ledger and sources actually inspected

The article has no direct research quotations. The following records methods and limits, including sources whose full text required direct retrieval rather than the web parser. Research files were temporary and were not added to the repository.

1. **Gravity: observation plus physical model.** Read the [EHT 2019 Paper VI](https://pure.uva.nl/ws/files/50944584/First_M87_Event_Horizon_Telescope_Results_VI.pdf), including interferometric observables, geometric and GRMHD comparisons, inferred shadow scale and mass, and uncertainty discussion. The reconstruction depends on sparse radio data and emission modeling. Approximately 55 million light-years follows its adopted 16.8 Mpc distance. Read [Donoghue's quantum-gravity effective-theory review](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2211.09902): low-energy quantum gravity has calculable results, while the high-energy completion is unfinished. Removed the solar-system size claim and the assertion that Einstein predicted this exact image.
2. **Dark matter: lensing inference.** Read [Clowe et al. 2006](https://arxiv.org/pdf/astro-ph/0608407), including background-galaxy selection, shape correction, lensing reconstruction, bootstrap centroid errors and gas-versus-stellar mass estimates. Its offset is evidence of mass beyond the dominant gas component, not a chemical identification or exclusion of every imaginable gravity model. The [Chandra image record](https://chandra.harvard.edu/photo/2006/1e0657/) supplies the layer colors and full data credits. Removed the unnecessary universal five-to-one claim.
3. **Dark energy: observational inference within models.** Read [Perlmutter et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/astro-ph/9812133), including the 42 supernovae, brightness standardization, extinction, selection and evolution checks. Read the parameters and model assumptions in [Planck 2018](https://arxiv.org/html/1807.06209v4). The roughly two-thirds fraction refers to fitted energy density in the standard model. Removed the context-free 120-zero comparison, galaxy-by-galaxy acceleration wording and unsourced quotation.
4. **Hot Big Bang: historical model reconstruction.** Read the parameter-estimation methods and tables in the [nine-year WMAP cosmological analysis](https://lambda.gsfc.nasa.gov/product/wmap/dr5/pub_papers/nineyear/cosmology/Hinshaw_2013_ApJS_208_19.pdf). The 13.800 Gyr result combines WMAP with other data and assumes the fitted cosmological model. The page states both limits. The map is cleaned multi-frequency temperature data, not a camera view of a beginning. Removed the television-static percentage and claim that theory nails everything afterward.
5. **Cosmic rays: reconstructed event energy.** Read [Bird et al.'s full event analysis](https://arxiv.org/pdf/astro-ph/9410067), including shower geometry, fluorescence profile, detector simulation, atmosphere and systematic errors. Table 1 gives 320 EeV with combined +92/-94 EeV uncertainty; 320 EeV is about 51 J and approximately 93 EeV is about 15 J. The particle type is not established. Removed the 2023 Matthews quotation from this 1991 event. The baseball comparison is a kinetic-energy scale, not a measured impact with a baseball's momentum.
6. **Time: metrology plus a cosmological boundary-condition question.** Read [Marshall et al. 2025](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=959906), particularly quantum-logic operation, frequency comparison and Table I. The 5.5 × 10^-19 number is systematic fractional uncertainty, distinct from stability or an endurance test. The [NIST announcement](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2025/07/nist-ion-clock-sets-new-record-most-accurate-clock-world) confirms the date and aluminum-ion instrument. Also inspected [Bothwell et al. 2022](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=933368), its spatially resolved spectroscopy and gravitational-redshift figure; the old claim about moving this pictured clock a millimeter was removed. Read the low-entropy framing in [Carroll and Chen](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/0410270). Their proposed cosmological explanation is not presented as consensus or a proved solution.
7. **Quantum measurement: mechanism and interpretation.** Read [Schlosshauer's researcher review](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0312059), especially the definite-outcome problem, environment-induced decoherence and interpretation-dependent limits. Physical detector/environment coupling does not require a conscious observer. Suppressing interference does not alone select a unique outcome in every interpretation. The soap-film photograph demonstrates classical interference only.
8. **Turbulence: formal open problem and an illustrative pattern.** Read [Fefferman's official problem statement](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/navierstokes.pdf), including the smoothness/breakdown alternatives, positive viscosity, incompressibility, initial conditions and dimensional restrictions. The [Clay status page](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/navier-stokes-equation/) still lists it as active. Read the full [2024 painting study](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.03415) after direct PDF retrieval: it analyzes selected eddies and grayscale luminance spectra/structure functions. The image has no observed velocity field; a scaling resemblance cannot prove the regularity result. Read [MoMA's object account](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/79802), which identifies the 1889 canvas and cautions against a literal window-view account.
9. **Glass: kinetic description and competing theories.** Read [Biroli and Garrahan's perspective](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1303.3542), including falling out of equilibrium, relaxation slowdown and competing structural/dynamical accounts. The article describes amorphous solidity without claiming the microscopic glass-transition problem is completely solved. Removed the old window-age hook, unsupported optical-fiber tangent and sweeping Anderson quotation.
10. **Superconductivity: established mechanism and an incomplete calculation.** Retrieved and read the pairing account and assumptions in [BCS 1957](https://journals.aps.org/pr/pdf/10.1103/PhysRev.108.1175). Read the full [Cui et al. 2025 cuprate calculation](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-56883-x), including its embedding/CCSD method, implicit doping, pressure/layer results and limitations. The calculations omit phonons and long-wavelength orders and use numerical approximations. Those results constitute progress without establishing a unique complete mechanism for every copper-oxide material. Removed the universal MRI claim.
11. **Lightning: observed initial process and remaining model gaps.** Read [Rison et al. 2016](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms10721.pdf), its VHF interferometer, field-change observations, initial positive breakdown and figures. Read the initiation section, alternatives and uncertainty list in the [2025 NOAA-hosted modeling roadmap](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/70860/noaa_70860_DS1.pdf). The roadmap distinguishes hypothesized field-enhancement mechanisms from observations and notes missing direct evidence linking cosmic-ray showers to initiation. The page does not elevate a candidate to a universal trigger.
12. **Consciousness: restricted decoding and a philosophical argument.** Read [Nishimoto et al. 2011](https://cs.brown.edu/people/tdean/projects/cortex/course/suggested_reading_list/supplements/documents/NishimotoetalCB-11.pdf), including individually fitted encoding models, separate training/test clips, repeated test presentations and the external movie prior. Three participants were authors; reconstructions were approximate and constrained by the prior library and fMRI. Read [Chalmers's 1995 argument](https://consc.net/papers/facing.html). The philosophical challenge is reported as disputed, not an experimentally demonstrated inability to explain consciousness.
13. **Magnetoreception: molecular candidate.** The journal page for [Xu et al. 2021](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03618-9) did not expose the full methods. Retrieved the full [Oxford accepted manuscript and supplement](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid%3Ac1bedca7-c96d-414a-94a2-c9a14aab18fa/download_file?safe_filename=Xu_et_al_2021_Magnetic_sensitivity_of_cryptochrome--.pdf&type_of_work=Journal+article). Read purification, transient-absorption/field-switching methods, mutations, spin-dynamics simulations and in-vivo qualifications. Experiments used millitesla fields; Earth-strength sensitivity was modeled with changed reaction rates. The authors say eye-protein manipulations would be needed to establish the living-bird sensor. The original 1972 behavioral paper was accessible only through catalog/abstract material, so it is not represented as a full-text method check.
14. **Placebo: small trial and a separate mechanism experiment.** Read the full [2010 open-label IBS trial](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0015591): 37 placebo and 43 controls, matched supportive visits, persuasive rationale, three weeks. The 59% versus 35% adequate-relief answers were a secondary outcome about the preceding seven days at day 21. Patients knew their assignment; assessors were blinded. Missing outcomes used carried-forward observations. Capsules contained AVICEL, not sugar. The study cannot separate reporting, expectations and treatment ritual or establish a cure. Read [Zubieta et al. 2005](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6725254/), its PET opioid-receptor measurements and experimental pain manipulation. That mechanism evidence concerns some placebo analgesia, not a proved explanation of this IBS result.
15. **Bicycle: predictive mechanics.** Read the full [Kooijman et al. 2011 paper](https://arendschwab.com/assets/pdf/kooijman2011bicycle.pdf), its linear Whipple model, 25 parameters, designed counter-rotating wheels, negative 4 mm trail and perturbation-recovery experiment. The model predicted stability above 2.3 m/s; the paper also notes unmodeled shimmy and idealized contact mechanics. Removing gyro and positive trail establishes that neither is necessary for this self-stability. It does not invalidate the mechanics used to design the vehicle or claim every bicycle is stable at all speeds.
16. **Reality: conditional philosophical skepticism.** Read the translated [First Meditation](https://www.earlymoderntexts.com/assets/pdfs/descartes1641.pdf), including dreams, radical doubt and the deceiver. The modern simulation comparison is explicitly conditional on reproducing every possible observation. The article supplies no experiment showing a simulated or unreal world, nor a blanket assertion that every simulation model is untestable.
17. **Gödel: theorem with conditions.** Retrieved and visually inspected the [Meltzer translation of the original paper](https://monoskop.org/images/9/93/Kurt_G%C3%B6del_On_Formally_Undecidable_Propositions_of_Principia_Mathematica_and_Related_Systems_1992.pdf), including its system/proof definitions and printed page 57, Proposition VI. The scan has no extractable text. Original neither-direction unprovability uses omega-consistency. Read the conditions and constructive proof in [Salehi's 2022 paper, Theorem 2.3 and Proof 2.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.04502v2), which supports the later Gödel-Rosser consistency formulation. Cross-checked the [SEP conditions](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/goedel-incompleteness/) as a secondary explanation; the visible page links the original and the modern primary proof. The result is system-relative. An effectively non-enumerable complete theory is not covered by the claim about a stronger system.
18. **Deep learning: designed procedure and partial interpretation.** Read the actual [training/backpropagation chapter](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/mlp.html), retrieved as HTML because the web parser failed. Retrieved and read the full [2024 Scaling Monosemanticity report](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2024/scaling-monosemanticity/index.html), especially sparse-autoencoder fitting, behavioral interventions and limitations. The study reconstructs activations at one middle layer of Claude 3 Sonnet and reports at least 65% explained variance, not complete circuitry. Its reconstruction/sparsity objective is a proxy for interpretation. Removed the universal no-convergence-theory quotation and Nobel tangent rather than letting them support a claim of total ignorance. No claim of established AI consciousness remains.

Root and the read-only integration auditor supplied supplementary checks of the clock, IBS outcome, cosmic-ray uncertainty, quantum measurement, skepticism, Gödel conditions and AI feature analysis. Those checks were incorporated, while this editor retained ownership of the full article and report.
