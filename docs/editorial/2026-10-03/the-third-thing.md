# The Third Thing editorial review

Reviewed and revised on 2026-10-04. Assigned page: `archive/the-third-thing/the-third-thing.html`. The complete original article, captions, sources, metadata, CSS and inline scripts were read. There is no script-generated essay prose. AGENTS, VOICE, STYLE, the relevant CANON guidance, the editorial brief and individual assignment informed the revision. Quran and New Testament supplied the presentation reference.

## Reader payoff and scope

The reader can distinguish inherited risk from an individual prediction, understand what the service-dog comparison can support, and consider an argument for responsibility without being told that genetics has proved free will. The owner's affection for steady service dogs, belief in a moral order and hopeful view of change remain explicit personal judgments. The essay does not invent training experience, a remembered encounter or another owner's recollection.

The original argument depended on three category errors: population variance became a percentage of a person; dogs were declared incapable of choosing; and a historical estimate of nontransmission of abuse became a measurement of conscious refusal. The rewrite repairs those premises before making the ethical argument. It also removes the claim that research proves almost nobody changes before suffering.

## Sources actually accessed and methods checked

1. **S1: Bouchard et al., 1990, Science.** [Original seven-page paper mirror](https://www.psychologyib.com/uploads/1/1/7/5/11758934/bouchard_et_al._1990.pdf); [publisher identifier](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.2218526). Read the original paper's assessment, recruitment, variance model, contact and rearing discussion. Its focus included 56 monozygotic pairs raised apart, with differing subsamples by test. Recruitment was not random assignment of children to homes. Twins can share prenatal conditions and have contact. The authors distinguish genetic, environmental and measurement contributions and caution against dismissing parenting or education. Used for resemblance and design limits, not a universal personality or IQ percentage.
2. **S2: NIH MedlinePlus, heritability.** [Official genetics explanation](https://medlineplus.gov/genetics/understanding/inheritance/heritability/), read in full. Supports population, environment and time specificity and rejects percentage-of-a-person and immutability interpretations.
3. **S3: Morrill et al., 2022, Science.** [Original full-paper mirror](https://www.centrale-canine.fr/sites/default/files/2022-11/Ancestry-inclusive%20dog%20genomics%20challenges%20popular%20breed%20stereotypes.pdf); [publisher identifier](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.abk0639). Downloaded the original paper and read the relevant methods, breed definitions, factor construction, ANOVA, ancestry mixed model and discussion. The project surveyed 18,385 dogs and had genetic data for 2,155; these are not interchangeable analysis denominators. Confirmed purebreds were classified using registration or sequencing evidence, with inferred ancestry taking precedence when available. Across 78 confirmed breeds, generalized eta squared averaged 0.089 with SD 0.039, range 0.034 to 0.253, across the 110 questions and eight factors. The 6,797 figure in the figure's overall ANOVA pool is not the same as saying all those dogs were confirmed purebreds. A separate model of 1,002 admixed dogs with no breed above 45 percent ancestry estimated about 9 percent variance across behavioral factors while incorporating age and relatedness. Both analyses concern variation across measured outcomes. The main paper explicitly discusses owner and stereotype bias. The complete separate supplementary files and raw dataset were not independently reanalyzed; no claim is made to have done so.
4. **S4: Bray et al., 2017, PNAS.** [Original university-hosted six-page paper](https://cpb-us-w2.wpmucdn.com/web.sas.upenn.edu/dist/0/236/files/2017/04/Bray-et-al-PNAS-2017-1mmgewk.pdf), downloaded and read, including training procedures, behavior tasks, analysis and limitations. Maternal observations covered 21 mothers and 138 puppies. The outcome analysis used 98 dogs after exclusions, including 27 medical releases, one transfer and 12 with missing task data. Mothers reared their biological offspring; no randomized cross-fostering isolated maternal care. The authors expressly identify inherited temperament as a possible confound. Their measured problem-solving behavior also contradicts a blanket assertion that the dogs have no behavioral choices. The PMC route initially returned a bot gate; the university paper was the usable original source. No human parenting inference is retained.
5. **S5: AVSAB puppy socialization statement.** [Official two-page position statement](https://avsab.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Puppy-Socialization-Position-Statement-FINAL.pdf), both pages read. Its copyright date is 2008; the 2024 hosting directory is not a new experiment or publication date. It identifies the first three months as especially important, recommends safe exposure and continued socialization, and discusses continued training. Used for broad developmental guidance, not a claim that a missed window makes later learning impossible or a detailed vaccination protocol.
6. **S6: Kaufman and Zigler, 1987, American Journal of Orthopsychiatry.** [Publisher abstract and bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1939-0025.1987.tb03528.x) opened; the publisher full-text/PDF route was unavailable. The seven-page original article was read through a [third-party OCR rendering](https://www.scribd.com/document/1026649194/Kaufman-1987). This is an access limitation: the mirror is not author authenticated and was not treated as a fresh dataset. The original review argues for approximately 30 percent transmission, plus or minus five points, from heterogeneous studies and methodological judgment. It is not a pooled modern census. Its prospective/retrospective comparison illustrates denominator errors: in the Hunter and Kilstrom neonatal-intensive-care parent study, nine of 49 historically abused parents were identified as maltreating, whereas nine of ten identified maltreaters had reported abuse histories. Other studies differ in parent selection, abuse definitions and follow-up. The review does not measure 70 percent of all survivors consciously refusing abuse. Only the noninevitability conclusion is retained; no exact current rate depends on the OCR.
7. **S7: Widom, Czaja and DuMont, 2015, Science.** [Full original author manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5308058/) opened and methods/results read. The initial cohort contained 908 children with court-documented maltreatment in 1967-1971 and 667 matched comparisons from one Midwestern county. Later interviews and agency searches involved different available/resident subsets. Parent reports, offspring reports and child-protection records measure different things. Exposure to surveillance can influence recorded detection, and an agency allegation does not itself establish that the study parent perpetrated the act. Used for increased risk without inevitability and informant limits, without importing a new universal percentage. Access was intermittently gated on subsequent requests, after the full manuscript had been read.
8. **S8: Aristotle, Nicomachean Ethics, Book II.** [W. D. Ross translation](https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/nicomachaen.2.ii.html), opened and read, especially II.1 and II.4. Supports habituation together with knowledge, choice for the act's own sake and stable character. This is primary philosophical text, not evidence that genes or experience leave a distinct causal gap.
9. **S9: Romans 5.** [World English Bible primary text](https://ebible.org/engwebp/ROM05.htm), opened and read, with [Bible Gateway's WEB rendering](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans%205%3A3-5&version=WEB) as an additional check. The text relates suffering, perseverance, character and hope in the context of God's love. The essay paraphrases it. An older eBible URL returned 404 and was replaced with the accessible edition path.
10. **S10: Frazier et al., 2009, Psychological Science.** [Original author-uploaded paper and full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/26281342_Does_Self-Reported_Posttraumatic_Growth_Reflect_Genuine_Positive_Change); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2009.02381.x). Read the actual method, results and limitations, not just the abstract. Four university samples supplied 1,528 baseline respondents, with 1,281 completing the approximately eight-week follow-up. The relevant subset was 122 students reporting sufficiently distressing intervening events, after exclusions; it was mostly female and predominantly young. Retrospective growth reports were compared with before-and-after questionnaire scores. These are self-report measures, not objective assays of moral character. Some measured domains improved; most correlations between retrospective reports and measured change did not substantiate equivalence. The short interval and college sample limit generalization. The article is careful about questionnaires beside its claim.
11. **S11: Buck v. Bell, 274 U.S. 200, 1927.** [Library of Congress scan of original U.S. Reports](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/ll/usrep/usrep274/usrep274200/usrep274200.pdf), downloaded and read in full, including arguments and opinion. The decision date, vote and Holmes quotation appear in the original; the opinion occupies pages 205-208. This is direct evidence of the ruling and its discriminatory rationale. It does not independently diagnose the three women, document the exact reason for institutionalization, prove rape, establish Vivian's school record, count nationwide sterilizations or establish a Nuremberg defense. Attempts to open [the cited Cold Spring Harbor grade-book archive item](https://www.eugenicsarchive.org/html/eugenics/static/images/1232.html), its alternate host and image route did not return the document. No claim is made that the grade book was inspected. Those unsupported historical particulars were removed rather than inferred from Holmes.
12. **S12: U.S. Navy, Sully at Walter Reed.** [Official February 2019 release](https://www.navy.mil/Press-Office/Press-Releases/display-pressreleases/Article/2239048/sully-hw-bush-has-a-new-job-at-walter-reed-medical-center/), opened and read. Supports the service to Bush and transition to the Walter Reed program in 2019. Subsequent requests sometimes errored, but the initial original release was accessible. Existing photograph files and licensing credit were preserved.
13. **S13: Bhagavad Gita 2.47.** [Sanskrit, translation and commentary](https://www.holy-bhagavad-gita.org/chapter/2/verse/47/), opened and read, identified as Swami Mukundananda's rendering. Supports action without attachment to its fruits and avoidance of inaction. The essay names Krishna and Arjuna and identifies the author's narrower application. It does not equate all traditions' karma doctrines. IIT Gita Supersite search results were located, but the actual page failed to open; that route is not counted as a successfully read source.

## Structure, voice and useful length

The revised route moves once through inheritance, the dog, practice, pain, the court ruling and the author's belief. The Watson/Galton chronology, Jim twins anecdote, candidate-gene detour, broad parenting advice, antinatalism aside, quantum detour, repeated callouts and separate concluding speech no longer compete with that route. The legally verifiable sterilization example survives; unsupported historical totals do not. Sources become an optional native disclosure, while each substantive factual claim keeps a link in the main text.

All five VOICE passes were applied:

1. **Monotone reading:** removed card-game metaphors, dramatic commands to look closer, suffering as a door, personal-destiny formulas and the staged confession. Factual distinctions still hold without an orator's cadence.
2. **First-paragraph deletion test:** the original opening of advantaged cruelty versus deprived kindness was cut. The new opening was tested for deletion and retained because it supplies the reader's concrete stake and connects to the evidence, rather than warming up the title.
3. **Generic sentence pass:** replaced rhetorical subheads and abstract proclamations with specific study limits, the named guide-dog program, Aristotle's distinction and the stated owner belief. The wrapper remains an explicit illustration. No new autobiography was supplied.
4. **Ten-percent cut:** the first complete factual rewrite contained approximately 1,726 main-path words. A further pass removed duplicated interpretive conclusions, an unneeded callout and redundant caveats. The final 1,547 is approximately 10.4 percent below that rewrite and 45.0 percent below the original main path. No preset word target dictated the final length.
5. **Source pass:** numbers have sources; the competing explanation of causes, nonshared experience and measurement error appears in the text. Correlation, legal assertion, religious interpretation and belief have different confidence language beside the claims.

Approximate counts use HTML text nodes with contractions and compounds treated as words. The main-path boundary is the article through the final belief paragraph, before the appendix, including section headings, captions and the Holmes quotation. Hero metadata, photograph credits and source notes are reported separately. Navigation, footer, CSS and JS are excluded.
