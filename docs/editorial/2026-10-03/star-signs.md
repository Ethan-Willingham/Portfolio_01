# Star Signs, X-Rayed: editorial review

Completed 2026-10-04. The revised files are `archive/star-signs/star-signs.html`, `js/star-signs.js`, `js/star-signs-page.js`, and this review. No shared styles, index, progress file, assets, commits, or deployment were changed.

## Reader payoff and reading path

The reader can see how a birth chart's positions are calculated, distinguish three incompatible labeling conventions, change the house rule, and compare a feeling of personal recognition with a blinded matching result. The skeptical argument now rests on a specific astrology experiment and the limits of the demonstration.

The main path is the opening, chart introduction, three Sun-label conventions, Carlson's chart-to-profile test, and the final distinction between improving positions and establishing a personality prediction. The instrument and generic-reading exercise remain prominent. Calculation assumptions, the condensed historical timeline, two methodological explanations, and sources are optional. None of the essential limitations depend on opening a disclosure: approximation, boundary uncertainty, manual offsets, unknown time, and polar fallback appear beside the affected output.

## What was wrong and what changed

- The original called approximate computed positions the actual sky, a sidereal equal-sign label the real sky, and a fixed month/day table actual IAU membership. The new labels distinguish these models. Whole-degree readouts say about, with a visible caution near a boundary.
- The original Fagan-Bradley constant used a 1950 value at the wrong epoch. It has been corrected. The abbreviated day-count formula also gave January and February 1900 the wrong day. UTC calendar arithmetic fixes that independently of timezone rollover.
- Unknown-time mode continued calculating from the previously chosen hour. It now always uses local noon at the entered offset, explains that assumption, samples the day for changing signs, and omits rising sign and houses.
- An unlisted place could retain a previous city's coordinates. Blank offsets became zero. Invalid coordinates were accepted. Inputs now clear stale data, reject incomplete or out-of-range values, and clear misleading results. The UTC offset is visible rather than hidden behind coordinates.
- The house comparison could stay stale or print undefined houses. It now refreshes with chart changes and explicitly requires a known time and place. High-latitude or failed Placidus iterations label the actual Equal fallback in the note, comparison column, and copied summary.
- Generated prose claimed modern personalities were fixed by Ptolemy, and attributed the Scales to a Roman invention. All ten planet entries and all twelve sign entries were rewritten. The displayed keywords are examples of modern interpretation, separated from sourced historical rulerships or discovery records.
- The essay treated precession as an overlooked update, IAU boundary publication as an event that moved the sky, and newspaper astrology as a single invention younger than radio. Those claims were corrected beside their subjects. The R101 story and precise universal trait-origin dates were removed.
- The casino psychokinesis story was called astrology's strongest evidence, although it was not a natal-chart test. Broad claims about all lunar effects, sleep, human behavior, financial studies, and replication extended beyond the cited material. The entire detour was replaced with a directly relevant chart-matching experiment. No verdict about those omitted lunar or psi claims is retained.
- The opening used a near-quotation of Forer's sketch while simplifying its protocol. It is now a clearly identified custom paragraph. The original study's separate description is accurate, and the exercise makes no claim to measure the visitor's personality or experimentally refute natal astrology. Repeating it clears the earlier rating and reveal.
- Sharing no longer invents a reader's beliefs, treats a birthday guide as today's sky, or uses the wrong page address. It copies the actual chart assumptions and approximate signs. Clipboard success is reported only after the browser reports success, with visible selectable text after failure.

## Residual limits

This remains a compact demonstration, not a professional ephemeris or timezone service. It uses a UTC-like time input without a historical UT/TT model, simplified orbits, rounded city coordinates, a linear sidereal offset, a coarse constellation calendar, and longitude-only house assignments. Unknown-time sampling can miss a brief crossing and cannot recover an instant. The quarter-degree caution band is an editorial warning, not a certified error guarantee. Near a sign or house boundary, higher precision can change the label. High-latitude horizon geometry remains difficult even when Equal cusps can be drawn.

Modern keywords are examples rather than a complete survey of astrological schools. Ancient house reconstruction is contested. Carlson is a specific experimental result, not the final study of every practice, and the on-page generic reading is not a controlled study. No claim about astrology's universal falsification or all lunar effects remains.
