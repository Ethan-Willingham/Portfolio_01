# Individual editorial assignment

Treat the assigned post as a complete standalone request. The owner asked for a
thorough review and revision: how can it be better, is it accurate, is it too long,
and how can it lose the generic assistant voice? Work at GPT-6.1 Sol extra-high
reasoning. Completion is based on the quality of the result, without artificial
delays or a preset word limit.

Read `AGENTS.md`, `VOICE.md`, `STYLE.md` and the relevant parts of `CANON.md`.
Study `quran.html` and `new-testament.html` for presentation. The writing should
sound like a knowledgeable person explaining the particular subject. Specific
facts, mechanisms and honest judgments carry the prose. Repeated dramatic framing,
staged reader instructions, decorative metaphors and generic moral certainty do
not earn their space. Existing personal experience and owner beliefs may stay,
clearly identified. Do not invent memories, expertise, observations or research.

## Work required

1. Read the entire post, including sources, disclosures, captions and prose
   generated by scripts. Map its main reading path, optional reference material
   and interactive features. State the concrete payoff for the reader.
2. Build a claim ledger. Distinguish observation, model, interpretation, personal
   belief and illustrative example. Check every central factual claim and every
   consequential number against sources actually opened. Browse for medical,
   financial, niche, uncertain and changing claims. Prefer original papers,
   official guidance, archives, museums and primary texts. Read source methods
   and limits, rather than treating a search snippet or abstract as proof.
3. Correct overclaims beside the claim itself. A caveat at the end cannot repair
   an inaccurate headline, diagram, calculator or opening paragraph. Correlation
   does not establish a cause; a model does not establish an outcome. Omit claims
   that cannot be responsibly supported. Keep substantive source links beside
   their claims and use a compact source section where useful.
4. Decide what belongs in the main path and what deserves optional detail. Cut
   repeated explanations, weak tangents and multiple endings. Follow all five
   VOICE passes. Assess a ten-percent cut, then justify the resulting length in
   the review. A long reference can remain long if the main path is manageable.
5. Rewrite sentence by sentence. Warm plain language, inline definitions,
   contractions where natural, specific sentence-case headings and varied rhythm.
   Preserve substance rather than turning the whole post into slogans. End on a
   fact, a limit or an honest open question. Accuracy takes priority over old
   voice calibration examples when they contain an overclaim.
6. Match the sacred-book presentation using the shared stylesheet contract below.
   Keep working tools, data, captions, images, credits, archive URLs and banners.
7. Synchronize the document title, social title and visible h1. Update descriptions,
   standfirst and honest reading-time metadata. For large references, make clear
   whether the estimate covers the main path or the whole reference. Give a
   proposed archive-card description of about 25 to 30 words in the review.
8. Check HTML structure, inline JavaScript syntax, local asset references and any
   changed widget behavior. Inspect your diff. Root will perform the combined
   browser and independent review, but report what you personally verified.

post's existing inline styles and other CSS, immediately before `</head>`.
Add `editorial-page` to the body's existing class list. Keep the archive-banner

reading metadata is `p.u-meta`. A scroll cue, if retained, gets `editorial-scroll`.
Remove conflicting inline styles. Place these in title, standfirst, metadata
order. Move any opening image just after the hero rather than placing the image
above the title or including it in the vertical-centering calculation. Preserve
image classes and hooks. The shared hero supplies centered placement, shared
gutters, the religious-post title scale and a near-viewport opening. If a existing
parent constrains its width, adjust that page's local wrapper safely.

Plain reading columns should follow the same roughly 64-character measure and
Century Supra at about 1.27rem with 1.72 line height. Major sections use fine rules,
centered serif headings and generous breathing room. Preserve dedicated UI fonts,
tables, charts, meaningful accents and specialized visual layouts. Mobile uses
the existing responsive gutter token, exactly 20px at a 375px viewport. Do not
duplicate wrapper gutters. Do not recolor photographs or print styles. The
photography gallery's neutral background and scale are specifically protected.
