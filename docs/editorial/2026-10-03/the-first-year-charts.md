## Corrections made during integration

## Original sources independently checked

- [CDC PRAMS analysis](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/69/wr/mm6919a2.htm): read the symptom definition, response criteria, weighting, timing and relevant table rows.
- [Paulson and Bazemore 2010](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/185905): read the original PDF's inclusion and pooling methods, results and subgroup limits. The old link led to an unrelated article.

- [CDC breastfeeding results](https://www.cdc.gov/breastfeeding-data/survey/results.html) and [methods](https://www.cdc.gov/breastfeeding-data/survey/methodology.html): checked the 2022 column, uncertainty and household survey design.
- [NCHS historical dataset](https://data.cdc.gov/National-Center-for-Health-Statistics/NCHS-Infant-and-neonatal-mortality-rates-United-St/epev-k6ss): inspected the original JSON records for every plotted historical point. The historical race classification was checked against the original published table, without claiming to have reviewed the entire historical volume.
- Hill 2005, [original identifier](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16263986/): read the complete-paper mirror's recruitment, diet procedures, outcome definition, Table 2 and limitations. Sung 2018, [original identifier](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29279326/): read the full-paper mirror's multilevel analysis, baseline adjustment, main results and feeding-subgroup limits. - [NCHS final 2022 leading causes](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/164020/cdc_164020_DS1.pdf): read the data and ranking methods, ages-one-to-four Table 1 rows and infant Table 2 rows. This establishes the corrected denominators and counts; it does not constitute a review of every row in the 117-page report.
- [Shapiro-Mendoza and colleagues 2023](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/127170/cdc_127170_DS1.pdf): read period-linked methods, results, diagnostic-classification limits and Table 1. The published 2015 total is preserved despite the rounded component sum.
- [LEAP original paper mirror](https://media.mycme.com/documents/115/du_toit_2015_28582.pdf): read recruitment, randomization, assessment, outcomes, statistical methods and the greater-than-four-millimeter exclusion. [LEAP-Trio author manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12875682/): read the correct downloaded full text's enrollment, outcome classification, analysis and results. Some browser routes were gated. An earlier temporary file named `leap.txt` contained a different radiology paper and was excluded from the evidence review.

[Park 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-022-07762-8): read the correct full-text analysis and results, including the outcome-specific pool rather than only the abstract's ten-study total.

The Gradisar full paper remained unavailable. No inaccessible numerical result is reconstructed. Sources were checked selectively for the retained claims; original datasets were not reanalyzed and this guide was not clinically validated.
