# Seven Habits editorial review

## Reader payoff

A reader can distinguish the jobs of the seven habits, use the urgent/important matrix to examine a week, and try Covey's listening practice without treating his advice as a guarantee of success. The article retains every habit, the book's character argument, its definition of effectiveness, the trust metaphor, the funeral and woodcutter exercises, and the animated staircase.

## Original issues

The original opened on a supposed settled history of American success writing. Covey describes his own survey and interpretation; the post turned that interpretation into fact. It also dismissed communication techniques more absolutely than Covey does.

The order of the habits became an absolute prerequisite for cooperation, including in the diagram caption. Covey explicitly allows practicing the public habits before perfecting the private ones. Habit 1 treated choice as equally available and expanding influence as automatic. Habit 3 implied that scheduling exercise determines whether someone has a heart attack. That line was both unsupported and unfair.

The emotional bank account became the entirety of a relationship. Habit 5 substituted an invented rule about arguing another person's case better before speaking. Habit 6 made a mathematical metaphor sound like a plain fact. Habit 4 omitted Covey's own qualification about the availability of no deal.

The last section supplied an eye-roll, an answer to the eye-roll, a favored-habits ending, and another whole-book ending. Sales and accolade copy added prestige without helping the reader use the ideas. The hero was small and left aligned, while the reading column used the site's UI body face. On phones, the whole staircase shrank with its labels.

## All five VOICE passes

1. Monotone pass: replaced the sweeping historical opener, absolute sequence claims, moral blame, mathematical slogan, and broad claims about what most people do with named models and specific mechanisms.
2. Delete-the-opening pass: the original historical paragraph could disappear without losing the practical payoff, so it did. The replacement introduces the urgency/importance distinction through a phone call and preparation work.
3. Generic-sentence pass: removed the staged eye-roll exchange, corporate-wallpaper verdict, universal failure claim, exaggerated listening payoff, and whole-book-in-a-line callout. Added a concrete listening response, the full quadrant table, and a provisional third alternative.
4. Ten-percent pass: main-path volume falls by approximately 11 percent. The cut comes chiefly from the repeated verdicts, inflated transitions, and certainty rather than from the habits or source mechanisms.
5. Source pass: placed qualifications with the relevant models; linked original passages for history, sequence, and no deal; linked primary explanations for the habits; separated advice, owner judgment, illustration, and empirical evidence. The final substantive paragraph ends on constrained choice.
