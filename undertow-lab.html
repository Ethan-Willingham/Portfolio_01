Resolution and seed take effect on Restart. The fluid domain is a periodic rectangle, 2 by 1 in dimensionless units. The display fits it to the canvas.

Measurements are preparing.

The curls come from a two-dimensional fluid. A steady drive feeds its motion; pressure keeps it from compressing. Dye moves with that flow, stretching into thin ribbons as neighboring patches take different paths. Dragging pushes the fluid and adds more dye.

This is a fluid experiment in dimensionless units. The colors show which dye dominates locally, with contrast added to separate mixed patches. Lighting makes the edges easier to see.
