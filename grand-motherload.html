Touch: use the direction pad to move and fly. Tap Items for equipment. Tap SCOOP to collect water as you move, or DUMP to empty the whole tank and launch the rig. Inside the banya, drag coal from the bunker onto the grate, or tap to drop a chunk. The forge makes a reusable steel striker from two iron: heat it, hammer it, then quench with 2 L of water. Mine stone for flint. Use flint and steel to light the boiler; bellows feed the fire. In Bath, tap ADD WATER and then a ready guest bubble.
Controller: left stick to move, A to fly, Start to pause.

Sluice is my 2D mining game, free forever. Dig down, collect ore, sell it for fuel and upgrades, go deeper. No win screen, just a deeper hole and a heavier haul. Built almost entirely with LLMs, and still early.

            
Curious how it is built? There is a write-up on the about page.
